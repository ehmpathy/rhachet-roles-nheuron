# philosophy: the external brain

## .what

**nheuron = neuron + externalized.**

an external brain is cognition that lives outside the skull: memory that persists beyond a
session, patterns that compound over years, and reach that extends past one person's attention.

this repo is not a notes app and not an assistant. it is the substrate of a second brain — one
whose neurons happen to be files, roles, and clones rather than cells.

it **emanates from your wetware**. zoom: `._.zoom=wetware-bandwidth`

it must be **locked down** — a fort knox made of fort knoxes. zoom:
`._.zoom=fort-knox-of-fort-knoxes`

## .why externalize at all

the biological brain is extraordinary and badly bounded:

| the biological brain | the external brain |
|----------------------|--------------------|
| memory decays; details fade in days | recall stays exact, indefinitely |
| a session ends and its context evaporates | context persists across every session |
| patterns must be re-derived each time | patterns crystallize once, then compound |
| attention is scarce and serial | attention is directed, and parallel |
| knowledge dies with the person | knowledge outlives any one session or person |
| neurons cannot be shared | neurons can be shared |

the last row is the one with no biological counterpart. you cannot hand someone your synapses.
you can hand them a role, a brief, a clone.

## .the neuron is the wrong unit — the connection is the right one

a single neuron computes almost none of what a mind does. a brain is not a pile of neurons; a
brain is the **connections between them**. cognition is an emergent property of linkage, not of
storage.

so the external brain's value is NOT in how much it captures. a perfect archive nobody
traverses is a corpse, not a brain. the value is in what connects to what:

- capture alone = storage
- capture + linkage = cognition

this is why `connector` exists as a first-class role rather than a search box. the weave IS
the intelligence.

## .the jellyfish: no center, by design 🪼

the mascot is a design claim, not decoration. a jellyfish thinks **without a central brain** —
its cognition is distributed through a nerve net, it senses through the whole body, and it has
survived 500M+ years on that architecture.

the external brain is built the same way:

- **no center** — no single process must be alive for thought to persist; the brain is the
  files and the roles, not any one runtime
- **distributed intake** — thought arrives from wherever it drifts: email, texts, slack, paper
- **drift with the currents** — the brain follows where attention actually goes, rather than
  a rigid schema decided up front

a centralized second brain has a single point of failure and a single point of taste. a
distributed one degrades gracefully and grows sideways.

## .the loop: capture → connect → direct

the three roles are not a feature list. they are the cognitive loop, externalized:

| role | glyph | the cognitive act | maximizes |
|------|-------|-------------------|-----------|
| **collector** | 🫙 | perception — take thought in from everywhere | no thought lost |
| **connector** | 🧵 | association — weave new threads across old | emergent insight |
| **allocator** | 🔦 | attention — shine on what matters now | clarity on what's next |

perceive, associate, attend. that is a mind's inner loop, and each role externalizes one turn
of it. the `gatherer` role feeds the first turn — every mail it collects is raw perception,
awaiting linkage.

a loop that only ever collects is a hoard. a loop that only ever connects is a daydream. the
three compose or none of them pays off.

## .the clone is one connection into it

each clone — each **`one`** — is a single neuron connection into the external brain. that is
the literal etymology of the term (`clone` → `one`), and it states the relation exactly:

> a `one` is not the brain. a `one` is your synapse into it.

so `jelly@one.nheuron.com` reads as *jelly, a clone, one connection into the external brain*.
the mailbox is the address of that synapse — how you reach in, and how it reaches back.

this is why a clone warrants a real address rather than an invocation. you do not invoke a
synapse; you correspond across it.

## .externalized thought should be shareable

a biological brain cannot lend its neurons. an external one can — so it should.

patterns that compound for one person compound harder across many. a role hardened by someone
else's failures is a failure you never have to suffer. this is the whole case for the roles
being open: not charity, but compound interest on shared cognition.

> let's extend our reach. mutually.

## .the caveat

**it emanates FROM the wetware** — so the wetware stays the origin. the external brain extends
capacity (exact recall, durable patterns, tireless intake, parallel attention); it does not
originate judgment, taste, or intent. those emanate outward from the human, and every role here
answers to one.

this is continuity, not replacement. a wider seam means more of you reaches further — never
that something else takes the wheel. an external brain that starts to originate intent has
stopped extending a person and started substituting for one, which is a different project than
this.

the failure mode to avoid meanwhile is mundane: a brain that accumulates faster than it
connects. capture is cheap and feels productive; linkage is the work. when in doubt, weave
rather than hoard.
