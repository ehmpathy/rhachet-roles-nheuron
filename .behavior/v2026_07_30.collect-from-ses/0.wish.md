wish =

enable folks to provision the resources required to **send** and **read** email for a
domain they own.

## the shape

- **aws side** (ses, s3, iam) is declared + applied via **declastruct-aws** — idempotent
  get+set, the same way declastruct-aws provisions its own `/aws/` resources
- **cloudflare dns** is a one-time manual step by a human (mx, verification, dkim, spf)
- auth is plain **iam** via `AWS_PROFILE` (from keyrack) — no oauth, no per-mailbox
  delegation, no token expiry

## generalize

- the resources are generic, parameterized by a **mail-domain** and a **mailbox** — any
  domain owner reuses them, not just nheuron
- **subdomains are the primary case** — a dedicated mail subdomain like `mail.ehmpath.com`,
  not the apex (keeps the apex mx free, isolates blast radius)
- for tests, use **`demo.ehmpathy.com`** as the mail-domain

## the first caller

nheuron's gatherer `ses` mechanism — `rhx gather.mail --from ses --address robot@mail.ehmpath.com`
— reads mail once the infra stands up. the respond path rides the same identity.
